# CRM Sales Analysis – Power BI

## About the Project

This project is a CRM sales analysis dashboard I built using Power BI to understand sales performance and customer behavior.

The goal was to take the raw sales data and turn it into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to see where sales are coming from, which products are performing well, which customers are contributing the most, and where there are opportunities for improvement.

I used Power Query to prepare the data, DAX to create the necessary calculations, and Power BI to build the final dashboard.

## What I Worked On

For this project, I:

* Analyzed overall revenue and order performance.
* Compared sales across Kano, Lagos, and Abuja.
* Analyzed sales performance by product.
* Identified high-value and low-value customers based on their spending.
* Identified customers with low purchasing activity who could be considered at risk.
* Analyzed customer spending patterns to support customer segmentation.
* Created tables to compare customers based on their average spending.
* Built an interactive dashboard using KPIs, charts, tables, and filters.
* Used the results of the analysis to develop business insights and recommendations.

## Key Findings

Some of the main findings from the analysis were:

* Kano recorded the highest regional revenue at approximately ₦18.2M.
* Transformers had the highest product sales at approximately ₦25M, followed by Solar Panels at ₦20M.
* High-value customers contributed a significant portion of the overall revenue.
* Some customers had relatively low spending or purchasing activity and were classified as at-risk customers.
* Abuja recorded the lowest revenue among the three regions analyzed.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I identified several areas the business could focus on:

* Maintain the strong sales performance in Kano while investigating the reasons behind the lower performance in Abuja.
* Continue focusing on high-performing products such as Transformers and Solar Panels.
* Use personalized offers and loyalty strategies to retain high-value customers.
* Use targeted promotions and product recommendations to encourage low-value customers to increase their spending.
* Re-engage customers with low purchasing activity to encourage repeat purchases.
* Review lower-performing products to understand what may be affecting their sales.
